# Spanish SEO Rules

## Purpose

These rules prepare Spanish SEO copy for a future `/es` launch. They do not enable Spanish routes, hreflang, sitemap entries or Spanish canonicals.

## Title Guidance

- Prefer natural Spanish titles around 45-60 characters when possible.
- A longer title is acceptable if it remains readable and precise.
- Use `Brenych Studio` where brand trust helps.
- Keep case names unchanged.
- Avoid repeated keywords like "diseño web Barcelona" or "landing pages premium" across every page.

## Meta Description Tone

- Aim for 140-155 characters where possible.
- Write restrained, specific descriptions that explain the page's purpose.
- Mention proof, systems, premium front-end work, product surfaces or immersive interfaces when relevant.
- Avoid exaggerated claims and generic agency language.

## No Keyword Stuffing

Do not force combinations such as:

- agencia web premium Barcelona
- diseño web moderno Barcelona
- mejores landing pages
- desarrollo web profesional barato
- soluciones digitales integrales

Spanish SEO should strengthen the studio's position, not flatten it into a generic search listing.

## Barcelona / Spain Mentions

Mention Barcelona or Spain when:

- the page is about the studio's Spain-facing commercial position;
- the case is actually Barcelona-related, such as Barcelona Private Advisory or Oria House Barcelona;
- the service page is intentionally aimed at Spain-facing premium clients.

Do not mention Barcelona when:

- the case has no local relevance;
- it would create a fake local claim;
- the page is primarily about a product system, R&D platform, immersive archive or global web interface.

## Service Page SEO

Service pages should combine:

- the service type;
- the user or business context;
- the proof angle;
- the studio's premium interface-system positioning.

Example direction:

`Landing pages premium para lanzamientos enfocados, productos visuales y rutas de consulta privada.`

Avoid:

`Agencia de diseño web en Barcelona para todo tipo de páginas modernas.`

## Case Page SEO

Case pages should include:

- the case name;
- the system type;
- the proof or outcome;
- enough context to understand the page without over-explaining.

Example structure:

`CreatorOps - sistema de flujo de trabajo para creadores`

Meta descriptions should describe what the interface proves, not just what the screenshot shows.

## Technical Launch Rules

For MULTILINGUAL-01A:

- Do not add hreflang.
- Do not add Spanish sitemap URLs.
- Do not change canonical URLs.
- Do not enable `/es`.

For the later Spanish SEO launch:

- Add self-canonical Spanish URLs only when `/es` routes are public.
- Add hreflang `en` / `es` / `x-default` only after Spanish pages are complete.
- Add Spanish sitemap entries only after the routes are public and reviewed.
